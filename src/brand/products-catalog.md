## CHAINS

### Tier 1 — Durable Utility
**Material:** 316L Stainless Steel with PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) coating
**Finishes:** PVD Gold, PVD Silver
**Price:** $1.75/inch + $30 weld fee
**Zero-tarnish guarantee:** PVD coating is 3–5× harder than traditional plating
**Best for:** Active lifestyles, first-time permanent jewelry, budget-conscious luxury, gifting
**Chain Styles Available:** Box, Cable, Figaro, Rope, Snake, Curb
**Width options:** 1.5mm (delicate) / 2mm (classic) / 3mm (bold)

### Tier 2 — Refined Luxury
**Material Option A:** 14k Gold-Filled (solid gold layer bonded to base metal — 100× thicker than plating)
**Material Option B:** Argentium Silver (93.5% silver + germanium alloy — tarnish-resistant, hypoallergenic, brighter than sterling)
**Price:** $3.75/inch + $45 weld fee
**Best for:** Sensitive skin, daily wearers, those wanting lasting color without solid gold price
**Chain Styles Available:** Box, Cable, Figaro, Rope, Snake, Curb
**Width options:** 1mm (ultra-delicate) / 1.5mm (delicate) / 2mm (classic)

### Tier 3 — Solid Gold
**Material:** 14k Solid Gold (58.5% pure gold)
**Finishes:** Yellow Gold, White Gold
**Price:** $10.00/inch + $75 weld fee
**Best for:** Investment piece, heirloom quality, sensitive skin, milestone moments
**Chain Styles Available:** Box, Cable, Figaro, Rope, Snake, Curb
**Width options:** 1mm (ultra-fine) / 1.5mm (delicate) / 2mm (classic)
**Resale value:** Solid gold retains melt value. Client owns the metal.
