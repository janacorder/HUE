## Policies & Procedures

### Booking Policy
- $25 non-refundable deposit required to hold all appointments
- Balance due at session in full before welding begins
- Bookings via website only (no DM bookings)

### Cancellation & Rescheduling
- Cancel 24+ hours before: deposit credited to future appointment
- Cancel under 24 hours: deposit forfeited, no exceptions
- No-shows: deposit forfeited; must rebook with new deposit

### Age Policy
- 18+ for all services without restriction
- 16–17: guardian must be present and sign consent form
- Under 16: not available

### Allergy & Sensitivity Disclaimer
- Clients with known metal allergies must disclose before booking
- Tier 1 (stainless steel) is hypoallergenic but PVD coating may cause reactions in extremely sensitive individuals
- Tier 2 and 3 recommended for sensitive skin
- No liability assumed for undisclosed sensitivities

### Aftercare Instructions
1. Keep dry for first 24 hours after welding
2. Avoid submerging in pools, hot tubs, or ocean for 48 hours
3. Pat dry after showering — do not rub
4. Clean weekly with a soft cloth
5. Avoid harsh chemicals, chlorine, perfume directly on chain
6. Tier 1: PVD coating may wear over 1–3 years with heavy abrasion
7. Tier 2 & 3: Will last a lifetime with basic care

### Removal Policy
- Permanent jewelry is designed to stay on
- Removal requires cutting the chain — we do not weld rings shut, only chains
- We offer complimentary removal if client books a new piece same day
- Removal-only: $10 fee
