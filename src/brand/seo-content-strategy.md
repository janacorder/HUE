## On-Page SEO Checklist (Per Page)

- [ ] H1 contains primary keyword naturally
- [ ] Meta title: 50–60 chars, includes keyword + brand
- [ ] Meta description: 150–160 chars, includes CTA
- [ ] Image alt text: descriptive + keyword where relevant
- [ ] Internal links: each page links to at least 2 others
- [ ] Schema markup: LocalBusiness on home, FAQPage on Education, Product on Collections
- [ ] Page speed: images compressed, lazy-loaded
- [ ] Mobile: tested on iPhone and Android

## Conversion Rate Optimization

### Homepage
- Hero CTA above the fold on mobile — "Design Your Chain" is primary
- Trust stats after hero (social proof before scroll)
- Email capture: offer the free measurement guide (lead magnet)

### Design Your Chain Page
- Step indicator / progress bar keeps users moving forward
- Live price update creates commitment (anchoring effect)
- Mockup visualization increases confidence + reduces returns
- "Deposit $25 to hold your spot" — small ask, big commitment

### Services Page
- Anchor price (Party Favor per-person) makes individual sessions feel accessible
- Host benefit callout ("You always get a free chain") makes pop-up hosting irresistible
- Embedded form per service type — low friction, capture intent immediately

### Education Hub
- Internal links to Design Your Chain after every FAQ answer
- "Send me the printable guide" email capture = list building on educational intent
- Authority builds trust before purchase decision

### Cart / Checkout
- Show savings: "You saved $6 with the Glow & Glint Set"
- Gift box upgrade at checkout: impulse buy, high-margin
- "Add a charm?" upsell between configurator and booking
