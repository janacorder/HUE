### Re-Weld Service
**Price:** $20 flat (Tier 1 & 2) / $35 (Tier 3)
When a chain breaks at the weld point under normal wear within the first 30 days: complimentary re-weld. After 30 days or physical damage: flat re-weld fee. Client must bring original chain intact. New chain section if lost: material cost + weld fee.

### Chain Lengthening / Resizing
**Price:** Material cost for added length + $15 service fee
Client wants chain 1–2" longer (e.g. pregnancy, weight change). We cut, add a link section, re-weld. Only available if original metal matches our current stock.

### Charm Swap Service
**Price:** $10 per swap
Remove an existing charm (requires cutting the chain at charm attachment), add new charm, re-weld. Chain stays on body throughout. Available in-studio only.

### Stacking Session
**Price:** Standard pricing per chain × number of chains
Book time to weld 2–5 chains in a single session. Stacking discount: 3+ chains same session = 10% off total. Requires 60-min appointment block.

### Virtual Consultation
**Price:** Free (15 min)
For clients who want to plan their design before visiting. Video call via Zoom or FaceTime. We review tier options, chain styles, measurement instructions. Book via Wix Bookings — select "Virtual Consult."
