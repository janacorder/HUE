Basics

What is permanent jewelry?

Permanent jewelry is a bracelet, anklet, necklace, ring, or other body adornment that is welded directly onto your body with no clasp — and no way to remove it without cutting it off. It stays through showers, workouts, beach trips, sleep, and years of daily life.

If you've noticed a chain on someone's wrist with no visible clasp — seamless, uninterrupted, like it grew there — that's permanent jewelry. The concept has existed in various forms for centuries. The modern version, using a micro-pulse argon welder, has exploded in popularity over the last five years.

At HUE Glint, permanent jewelry is offered in three metal tiers: 316L stainless steel with PVD coating (Tier 1), 14k gold-filled and Argentium silver (Tier 2), and 14k solid gold (Tier 3). Every chain is sized to your exact body and welded in the studio or at a mobile event.

The Process

How the weld works.

The tool used in permanent jewelry is a micro-pulse argon welder — a precision instrument that delivers a controlled burst of energy to fuse two chain ends together. The process uses argon gas as a shielding element to prevent oxidation at the weld point, keeping the metal clean and the join strong.

The jeweler holds both ends of the chain at the weld point — a small distance from the skin — and activates the welder for less than one second. You will see a small flash of light. That's the argon arc, not fire. You will feel nothing.

The weld itself is often stronger than the chain links. If a chain ever breaks from stress or impact, it almost always breaks at a chain link — not at the weld. This is by design: the chain sacrifices itself to protect the skin.

Step by step at your appointment

1. Choose your location. We discuss where you want to wear the chain and talk through sizing considerations specific to that body part.

2. Select your tier, style, and charms. We lay out your options. You choose the metal tier, chain style, width, and any charms you want permanently attached.

3. Measure. We measure the exact circumference of your body location and add the appropriate ease for that location (wrist gets 0.75", ankle gets 1", etc.). The chain is cut to this exact length.

4. Weld. The chain is draped around your body and the ends held together. The micro-pulse welder fires. The chain is closed. The session is over.

5. Review and aftercare. We inspect the weld, review your care instructions, and send you off with branded packaging and a care card.

Tier 1 in 316L surgical-grade stainless steel is virtually indestructible. The PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) coating is 3–5× harder than traditional plating. This tier is right if you live in your jewelry and want zero maintenance.

Tier 2 in 14k gold-filled is not gold plating — by law, the gold layer must be at least 5% of total weight (100× thicker than plating). Body Locations

Where will you wear it?

HUE Glint offers permanent chains at 7 body locations. Each has different sizing requirements and fit considerations.

Wrist

Bracelet

The most popular location. Sits just below the hand, above the wrist bone. We add 0.75" ease for comfortable permanent wear. Average wrist measures 6–7" before ease.

Ankle

Anklet

Sits at the narrowest point of the ankle, just above the ankle bone. We add 1" ease — ankle chains sit slightly looser than bracelets to allow for movement.

Neck

Necklace

Three common lengths: Choker (14–16"), Princess (17–19"), Matinee (20–24"). Measurement method depends on the look you want.

Belly

Belly Chain

Measures around the natural waist at navel level while relaxed. We add 1.5" for comfortable drape. Belly chains are deliberately loose — measure generously.

Upper Arm

Armband

Measured around the widest point of the bicep with a slight flex. We add 0.5" for movement. Upper arm bands should slide on but not fall.

Finger / Toe

Ring

Measured at the knuckle — the widest point the ring must pass over. We add 0.25" for permanent comfort. Toe rings measure loosely around the toe base.

Measuring at Home

Get your exact fit.

You don't need a tape measure. You need a strip of paper or a non-stretchy piece of thread.

The paper method: Cut a strip of paper about 0.5" wide. Wrap it around your body location snugly — not tight, not loose. Mark where the paper meets itself. Remove and measure the marked length with a ruler. That's your circumference. We add the appropriate ease at your appointment.

If you use the online configurator: Enter your measured circumference. The tool adds the correct ease automatically based on your body location. The price displayed is based on your total fitted length.

Safety

Is permanent jewelry safe?

Yes. The micro-pulse argon welder produces a focused flash of energy at the metal tip — there is no heat transfer to skin, no open flame, and no risk of burns. The process is completely non-invasive and requires no numbing.

Metal safety by tier:

Tier 1 uses 316L surgical-grade stainless steel — the same alloy used in medical implants and body jewelry. It is nickel-free and safe for virtually all skin types.

Tier 2 uses 14k gold-filled (solid gold layer, not plating) and Argentium silver (germanium replaces the copper that causes most "silver allergies"). Both are hypoallergenic.

Tier 3 uses 14k solid gold — the most universally biocompatible jewelry metal available.

Age policy: Clients under 18 are welcome with a parent or guardian present and consenting. We do not weld minors without explicit adult consent and presence at the appointment.

MRI and medical imaging

Inform your medical team about any permanent jewelry before imaging. For most MRI procedures, the tiny amount of metal in a bracelet or anklet is not a concern. If removal is required, we cut it free at no charge and re-weld it when you're ready.

Aftercare

Keeping it perfect.

Permanent jewelry is low-maintenance — but the first 48 hours matter.

First 24 hours: Keep the chain completely dry. No showering, no pool, no ocean.

24–48 hours: You can shower. Pat dry after — do not rub. Still avoid submerging in pools, hot tubs, or salt water.

After 48 hours: Normal life. Shower, swim, sleep, work out with the chain on. Rinse after chlorine exposure. Avoid applying perfume, lotion, or sunscreen directly on the chain.

Chlorine: The one universal threat across all tiers. Rinse immediately if exposed to chlorinated water. For frequent swimmers, Tier 1 or Tier 3 are the most resistant options.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Does permanent jewelry set off airport security?
Almost never. The amount of metal in a permanent bracelet is far below the threshold that triggers airport metal detectors. The overwhelming majority of our clients travel frequently with no issues. In rare cases with highly sensitive full-body scanners, the chain may show visually but not trigger an alarm. You can inform TSA you have a permanent bracelet — they've seen it.
Can you shower with permanent jewelry?
Yes — after the first 24 hours. Keep it dry for the first 24 hours while the weld fully sets, then shower normally. Pat dry rather than rubbing. That's the whole point of permanent jewelry: it's yours through everything.
What happens if my chain breaks?
Bring it in — we'll re-weld it. The Re-Weld service is $20 for Tier 1/2 and $35 for Tier 3. Most breaks happen at chain links (not the weld) from unusual stress. If the weld fails within 30 days of your appointment, the re-weld is free — that's our weld warranty.
How do I remove permanent jewelry?
It must be cut off — that's the definition of permanent. Use small scissors, wire cutters, or come to us and we'll cut it for free. Removal takes about 30 seconds. Re-welding afterward is $20–35.
How long does the appointment take?
A single in-studio weld session takes about 20–30 minutes total. The actual weld is under 10 seconds. Most of the time is spent choosing your chain and getting measured — which is the enjoyable part.
Can I get permanent jewelry if I have a nickel allergy?
Yes. All three tiers at HUE Glint are nickel-free. 316L stainless is specifically formulated to be nickel-safe. 14k gold-filled, Argentium silver, and 14k solid gold are all hypoallergenic. If you have extreme metal sensitivity, we recommend Tier 3 solid gold — it's the most universally biocompatible option.
Does the weld ever come undone on its own?
No. A properly executed weld does not "come undone" — it is a physical fusion of metal. The only way a chain comes off is if a link breaks from stress (like catching on something), or if you cut it. Our 30-day weld warranty covers the rare case of an improperly executed weld.
What is the minimum age?
There is no minimum age, but clients under 18 require a parent or guardian present and consenting at the appointment. A parent signature is required on the consent form.
