Tier 1

Durable Utility

316L Stainless Steel · PVD Gold · PVD Silver

The workhorse of permanent jewelry. 316L surgical-grade stainless steel is corrosion-resistant, hypoallergenic, and virtually indestructible under daily wear. The PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) coating is 3–5× harder than traditional gold plating — it won't rub off, chip, or fade. Zero maintenance. Zero tarnish.

Best for: Active lifestyles · First-time permanent jewelry · Daily swimmers and gym-goers · Budget-conscious luxury · Gifting

Tier 2 — Most Popular

Refined Luxury

14k Gold-Filled · Argentium Silver (93.5%)

14k Gold-Filled is not gold plating. By law, the gold layer must be at least 5% of total weight — that's 100× thicker than standard plating. It looks, feels, and wears like solid gold at a fraction of the cost. Argentium Silver replaces copper with germanium, making it tarnish-resistant, brighter, and hypoallergenic. Both materials are ideal for sensitive skin.

Best for: Sensitive skin · Daily wearers · Lasting color without solid gold pricing · The "fine jewelry" look

Tier 3

Solid Gold

14k Yellow Gold · 14k White Gold (58.5% pure)

14k solid gold is 58.5% pure gold. It retains melt value — the gold in your chain is always worth something. It does not tarnish, does not fade, and is the most hypoallergenic material available. Solid gold permanent jewelry is an investment. It can be cut off, melted down, redesigned. It outlives trends, decades, and generations.

Best for: Investment pieces · Milestone moments · Most sensitive skin · Heirloom quality · Men's chains
