HUE Glint provides care instructions with every service. Following these instructions is the client's responsibility. We do not offer refunds on completed welds. If a weld breaks within 7 days of service under normal wear (not due to physical impact or submersion), we will re-weld at no charge with proof of purchase and appointment.
